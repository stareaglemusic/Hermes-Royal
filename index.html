Our story

Built in the Bronx, one job at a time.

Hermes Royal Inc. was founded in 2014 by Hermes Hoxha, who learned the trade working with established New York City heating and cooling contractors before setting out to build a company of his own.

The idea was simple: give New Yorkers a contractor who answers the phone, shows up when promised, and takes responsibility for the result.

“You point it, we solve it.”

More than a decade later, we serve homeowners, property managers, businesses and institutions across the Bronx, Manhattan and Westchester — fully licensed in New York City and Westchester County, EPA certified and insured.
